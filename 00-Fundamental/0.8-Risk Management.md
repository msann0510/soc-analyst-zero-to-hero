# Risk Management

---

# ⚠️ Risk Management

Risk Management is the process of identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and treating risks to keep them at an acceptable level.

The objective is to manage risk, not eliminate it completely.

---

# 📊 Risk Assessment
Risk Assessment evaluates the severity of a risk by considering:
- Likelihood
- Impact
The result helps organizations prioritize security issues.

---

# 🎲 Likelihood
Likelihood represents the probability that a threat will occur.
Higher likelihood generally increases the overall risk level.

---

# 💥 Impact
Impact measures the potential damage if a risk becomes a real incident.
Examples include:
- Financial loss
- Data breach
- Service disruption
- Reputational damage

---

# 📈 Risk Matrix
A Risk Matrix combines Likelihood and Impact to determine the overall Risk Level.
Common categories include:
- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

# 🎯 Risk Appetite
Risk Appetite defines how much risk an organization is willing to accept while pursuing its objectives.

# 📏 Risk Tolerance
Risk Tolerance represents the maximum level of acceptable risk before corrective action becomes necessary.

# ⬆️ Escalation
Escalation is the process of forwarding a security issue or incident to the appropriate team or authority when additional expertise or authority is required.

---

# 🛠️ Risk Treatment
Organizations generally handle risks using four strategies:

## 🛡️ Risk Mitigation
Reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.
## 🚫 Risk Avoidance
Eliminate the activity that creates the risk.
## 🤝 Risk Transfer
Transfer part of the risk to another party, such as an insurance provider or managed security service.
## ✅ Risk Acceptance
Accept the remaining risk because additional controls are unnecessary or not cost-effective.

# 📉 Residual Risk
Residual Risk is the remaining risk after security controls or treatment strategies have been implemented.
Residual Risk can be reduced but rarely eliminated.

---

# 🛡️ Common Security Controls
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Password Policies
- Patch Management
- Firewalls
- Encryption
- Security Awareness Training
- Access Control
- Backup

---

# 📌 Key Takeaways
- Risk cannot always be eliminated.
- Risk Assessment uses Likelihood and Impact.
- Risk Matrix helps prioritize security issues.
- Organizations choose different Risk Treatment strategies based on business needs.
- Residual Risk always remains after treatment.
